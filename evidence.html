We separate verified facts, analytical judgements, design hypotheses and aspirations. Every material claim should be traceable to the evidence that supports it.

The claims standard

Say precisely what the evidence permits.

Public-facing material should distinguish organizational capability from commissioned work, contextual familiarity from delivery, and a proposed outcome from a verified result.

Claim classMeaningRequired supportPublic language
Verified factDirectly evidenced and reviewable.Primary document, public record, contract or validated dataset.May be stated directly with source and scope.
ContextRelevant experience, geography or subject familiarity.Named basis and clear boundary.“Experience in” or “contextual familiarity with”.
AnalysisA reasoned interpretation of available evidence.Sources, assumptions and method.Presented as an assessment, not a fact.
Design hypothesisA proposition to test through engagement or delivery.Logic, risks and validation plan.“We propose”, “could” or “subject to validation”.
Governance cycle

Evidence follows the decision.

01

Register

Record material claims, assumptions and source ownership.

02

Classify

Separate facts, analysis, hypotheses and aspirations.

03

Verify

Review authority, currency, scope and contradictions.

04

Publish

Use language calibrated to the evidence class.

05

Update

Revise the record as operating evidence changes.
